## 1. About V.E.R.A.

V.E.R.A. is an AI-assisted mental wellness platform for users aged 13 and above. It provides:

- Text, voice, and avatar-based AI conversations
- Voice-pattern emotion estimates
- Mood, sleep, breathing, diary, medication, and wellness tools
- Session summaries and personal activity history
- Curated and professionally assigned mental-health resources
- Risk indicators that can support early review by authorized professionals

V.E.R.A. supports English and Filipino. Use the **EN/FIL** control in the header or the language controls in the mobile menu to change the interface language.

## 2. Important Safety Notice

V.E.R.A. is a support and self-awareness tool. It is not a replacement for a licensed mental-health professional, medical diagnosis, treatment, medication advice, or emergency care.

Voice emotion results are AI estimates based on vocal patterns. They may be incomplete or inaccurate and should not be treated as a statement of a person's true emotional or medical condition.

If you or another person may be in immediate danger:

1. Stop using the AI session.
2. Contact local emergency services or a crisis service in your area.
3. Tell a trusted adult, guardian, family member, counselor, or healthcare professional.
4. Do not rely on V.E.R.A. as the only source of help.

## 3. System Requirements

For the web version, use:

- A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari
- A stable internet connection
- JavaScript and cookies enabled
- A working microphone for Voice AI and Avatar AI
- Audio output or headphones to hear spoken replies
- Permission to open downloads for PDF reports

For the best voice-recognition experience:

- Use a quiet room.
- Keep the microphone close without touching it.
- Speak naturally and clearly.
- Use headphones to reduce echo.

## 4. Creating an Account

1. Open V.E.R.A.
2. Select **Sign Up**, **Get Started**, or **Start Your Journey**.
3. Enter the requested personal and account information.
4. Use a Gmail address. V.E.R.A. currently accepts addresses ending in `@gmail.com`.
5. Create a password with:
   - At least 8 characters
   - At least one uppercase letter
   - At least one lowercase letter
   - At least one number
6. Re-enter the password.
7. Read and accept the Terms of Service.
8. Submit the registration form.
9. Check your email and complete account verification.

### Users under 19

V.E.R.A. requires guardian consent when the birthday entered indicates that the user is under 19.

1. Enter the guardian's name.
2. Enter the guardian's Gmail address.
3. A guardian contact number may also be entered.
4. Confirm that the guardian information is accurate.
5. Send the verification code to the guardian.
6. Ask the guardian to provide the six-digit code from their email.
7. Enter the code in V.E.R.A.
8. Complete registration after consent is approved.

Do not use another person's email or enter guardian details without their knowledge.

## 5. Signing In and Recovering an Account

### Sign in

1. Open the V.E.R.A. home page.
2. Enter the Gmail address used during registration.
3. Enter the password.
4. Select **Sign In**.

After successful sign-in, regular users are taken to the User Dashboard. Administrator and psychology accounts receive access to their role-specific area.

### Verify or resend verification

If the account is not yet verified, V.E.R.A. opens the verification screen. Enter the code sent by email. Use **Resend Verification** if the original code is missing or expired.

Check the Spam or Junk folder before requesting another code.

### Reset a forgotten password

1. Select **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter the registered email address.
3. Open the reset link sent by email.
4. Enter and confirm the new password.
5. Submit the form, then sign in with the new password.

## 6. Navigating V.E.R.A.

The main header contains:

- **Home** — opens the landing page or signed-in dashboard
- **AI Features** — opens Voice AI, Avatar AI, or Chat AI
- **Activities** — opens the wellness activity library
- **EN/FIL** — changes the interface language
- **Bell icon** — shows feature and appointment notifications
- **Token balance** — shows the tokens available for paid AI session types
- **Profile menu** — opens Profile, Feedback, or Logout

On a small screen, select the menu icon to display these options.

## 7. Using the User Dashboard

The dashboard is the signed-in user's starting point. It displays:

- A daily greeting and mood check-in
- The V.E.R.A. AI card and current sync indicator
- A vitality score based on recorded activity
- Shortcuts to Chat AI, Voice AI, Avatar AI, Diary, and Breathing
- Resources assigned by an authorized professional
- General recommended resources

### Record the daily mood check-in

1. Select a mood when prompted, or select a mood button on the dashboard.
2. The full Mood Tracker opens.
3. Choose the emotion that best matches the current experience.
4. Add an optional reason or note.
5. Save the entry.

Vitality and wellness indicators are engagement summaries, not medical scores.

### Open a resource

Select a resource card to open its first attached link in a new browser tab. Resources marked **Assigned to you** were selected for the account by an authorized administrator or psychology professional.

## 8. Using V.E.R.A.'s AI Features

All AI features require a signed-in account and internet access.

### 8.1 Chat AI

1. Open **AI Features > Chat AI**.
2. Type a message in the text box.
3. Select the send icon, or press **Enter**. Use **Shift + Enter** for a new line.
4. Wait for V.E.R.A.'s reply.
5. Select **New Chat** to begin a separate conversation.

The current browser keeps the active chat and session reference locally so it can restore the conversation after a page refresh. Signing out clears local session data.

### 8.2 Voice AI

Voice AI costs **2 tokens per started session**.

1. Open **AI Features > Voice AI**.
2. Confirm that at least 2 tokens are available.
3. Choose a conversation engine:
   - **V.E.R.A. Voice** uses V.E.R.A. AI, generated speech, and voice-pattern analysis.
   - **Hume EVI** provides a live voice conversation using the voice configured by that service.
4. When using V.E.R.A. Voice, select a voice companion.
5. Start the call.
6. Allow microphone access when the browser asks.
7. Speak naturally.
8. Use the microphone control to record or stop/analyze, as shown on the screen.
9. Review the transcript and any displayed voice-emotion estimates.
10. Use the mute control when needed.
11. Select the end-call button to finish.

If there are not enough tokens, complete eligible wellness activities to earn more before starting a session.

### 8.3 Avatar AI

Avatar AI costs **3 tokens per started session**.

1. Open **AI Features > Avatar AI**.
2. Confirm that at least 3 tokens are available.
3. Select a companion category.
4. Choose a guide or animal companion.
5. For a human guide, select an available outfit if desired.
6. Select **Start Session** or **Connect Now**.
7. Allow microphone access.
8. Select the microphone button and speak.
9. Select it again to stop recording and send the message.
10. Wait while the companion processes and speaks its reply.
11. Review the conversation history and voice-emotion chart when available.
12. Use **Change Agent**, **Change Companion**, or the end-call button to leave the session.

Do not close the page while a recording is being processed.

### Understanding voice-emotion analysis

The chart may show several estimated expressions with confidence scores. These results:

- Analyze voice patterns rather than a person's complete situation
- Can be affected by noise, microphone quality, accent, speech style, or illness
- Are not guaranteed to identify the user's true emotion
- Are not a diagnosis or clinical assessment

## 9. Using Wellness Activities

Open **Activities** and use the category filters to find Games, Breathing, Journaling, Sleep, Tracker, or Report tools. Some completed activities award tokens. The reward amount shown in the completion message is the amount credited for that activity.

### 9.1 Clipcard Game

1. Open **Clipcard Game**.
2. Choose a game mode.
3. Select cards to reveal them.
4. Match identical pairs.
5. Complete all pairs to finish the game and receive any displayed reward.
6. Return to the mode menu to play again.

### 9.2 Take a Breath

1. Open **Take a Breath**.
2. Select a breathing technique.
3. Select **Start**.
4. Follow the on-screen inhale, hold, and exhale timing.
5. Select **Stop** when finished.
6. Open **History** to review saved sessions and duration.

Stop the exercise if breathing becomes uncomfortable or causes dizziness.

### 9.3 Digital Diary

1. Open **Digital Diary**.
2. Write an entry.
3. Optionally choose a mood and color.
4. Select the save/drop button to place the entry in the Serenity Jar.
5. Select a saved entry to read it.
6. Select **Delete** and confirm to permanently remove an entry.

### 9.4 Sleep Tracker

1. Open **Sleep Tracker**.
2. Select the date on the calendar. Future dates cannot be selected.
3. Select the sleep time.
4. Select the wake time.
5. Review the calculated duration and status.
6. Select **Save Sleep Data**.
7. Open **Sleep History** to review previous records.
8. Use the delete icon and confirm to remove a record.

### 9.5 Mood Tracker

1. Open **Mood Tracker**.
2. Select an emotion from the feelings wheel or available mood choices.
3. Add an optional reason.
4. Select **Save Mood**.
5. Use **All**, **Week**, or **Month** to filter history and charts.
6. Select **Download PDF** to export the displayed period.
7. Delete one entry or use **Clear All History** when needed.

Deletion requires confirmation. Review the selected item or period before continuing.

### 9.6 Medication History

This feature records information only. It does not prescribe medication or replace instructions from a doctor or pharmacist.

1. Open **Medication History**.
2. Select **Log Medication Entry**.
3. Enter the medication name, dosage, frequency, and start date.
4. Indicate the relevant status or medication type shown by the form.
5. Optionally record effectiveness and notes for a psychiatrist.
6. Save the entry.
7. Review maintenance and previous medication sections.
8. Select **Export PDF** to download the medication history.

Never start, stop, or change medication based only on V.E.R.A.

### 9.7 Weekly Wellness Report

1. Open **Weekly Wellness Report**.
2. Choose **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, or **Year**.
3. Review mood, sleep, breathing, and other available activity summaries.
4. Select chart points to view details.
5. Select **Download PDF** to save the report.

The report becomes more useful as more activities are recorded.

## 10. Managing Your Profile and Privacy

Open the profile menu and select **Profile**.

### Edit profile information

1. Select **Edit**.
2. Update the allowed fields, such as name, username, birthday, gender, contact number, or guardian email where applicable.
3. Select **Save**.
4. Use **Cancel** to discard unsaved changes.

The account email cannot be edited from the profile form.

### Change the profile picture

1. Select the edit icon on the profile image.
2. Choose an image from the device.
3. Wait for the upload confirmation.

### Privacy tab

The Privacy tab contains:

- **Store Conversations** — permits eligible conversations to be stored with the account
- **AI Analysis** — permits eligible stored content to be analyzed
- **Detection: Always On** — indicates that mandatory safety/risk detection cannot be disabled

Changing a switch saves the preference immediately.

### Sessions tab

Use the Sessions tab to:

- Filter by Text, Voice, or Avatar
- Filter by risk level
- Sort newest or oldest first
- Review a session summary
- Review recorded messages when available

What appears here depends on the privacy permissions and data available for the session.

### Appointments tab

The Appointments tab displays appointments created through authorized professional notes, including the scheduled date, professional's name, and available observations.

## 11. Notifications, Resources, and Feedback

### Notifications

Select the bell icon to view:

- Scheduled appointment notifications
- Feature announcements

Select **Clear** to remove the displayed alerts from the current browser. Clearing an alert does not cancel an appointment.

### Feedback

1. Open the profile menu.
2. Select **Feedback**.
3. Choose the appropriate feedback or report type.
4. Enter the requested details and submit.

Use reports responsibly and provide enough factual detail for the team to understand the issue.

## 12. Administrator Guide

Administrator features are available only to accounts with the administrator role.

### Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard summarizes:

- Total users
- Active sessions
- Critical-risk indicators for the current day
- System status
- Session risk distribution
- Recent user registrations

Select a summary card to open its related section.

### User Management

1. Open **Admin > User Management**.
2. Search for a user or filter by role, status, age group, or sort order.
3. Use **View Sessions** to open the user's activity and session record.
4. Use **Edit** to update permitted account details or role.
5. Use **Add User** to create an authorized account.
6. Use **Delete** only when removal is intended, then confirm the action.

Deleting an account may remove access to associated data. Verify the selected user before confirming.

### User sessions and reports

From a user's session page, an administrator can:

- Filter sessions by AI type and risk level
- Sort by date or risk score
- Review activity and risk trends
- Open an individual conversation
- Review mood, sleep, breathing, medication, and other recorded activity
- Download a user PDF report
- Assign or remove curated resources

### Conversation review and professional notes

Open a session to review the conversation, summary, and available risk information. Authorized staff can add professional notes, observations, severity information, and a next appointment.

Use objective, respectful language. Do not place unnecessary sensitive information in notes.

### Resource Management

1. Open **Admin > Resources**.
2. Select **Create Resource**.
3. Enter its title, description, category, image information, and comma-separated links.
4. Save the resource.
5. Use **Edit** to update it.
6. Use **Delete** and confirm to remove it.

Check that external links are trustworthy and appropriate before publishing or assigning them.

### Reports

Open **Admin > Reports** to review aggregate user engagement, activity types, registrations, role and status distribution, session risk distribution, and users requiring closer review.

Select **Download PDF** to export the current report.

### User Feedback

Open **Admin > User Feedback** to:

- View feedback and submitted reports
- Filter the displayed type
- Mark an item resolved or pending

## 13. Psychology Professional Guide

Psychology features are available only to accounts with the psychology role.

The Psychology sidebar contains:

- **Dashboard**
- **User Management**
- **Resources**
- **Analytics**

### Dashboard and Analytics

Use the dashboard to review user totals, recent activity, session risk distribution, and critical indicators. Open **Analytics** for aggregate engagement and risk charts and to download available PDF reports.

### Review a user

1. Open **User Management**.
2. Find the user.
3. Open the user's sessions.
4. Filter by date, AI type, or risk level.
5. Review available wellness activity, conversation, summary, and risk information.
6. Open a session when detailed review is needed.

### Add notes and appointments

In an individual session:

1. Open the professional notes area.
2. Enter clinical observations and other requested fields.
3. Add a next appointment date if appropriate.
4. Save the note.

The user will see saved appointments in Profile and may receive a notification.

### Assign resources

1. Open the user's session/profile report page.
2. Go to **Assign Resources**.
3. Select one or more resources.
4. Select **Assign Selected**.
5. Confirm that the resources appear under **Assigned Resources**.

Assigned resources appear on the user's dashboard. Remove an assignment only after confirming it is no longer needed.

Risk labels and AI analysis are decision-support signals. They require professional judgment and should not be used as the sole basis for diagnosis or intervention.

## 14. Troubleshooting

### I cannot sign in

- Confirm that the email and password are correct.
- Use the registered Gmail address.
- Complete email verification.
- Select **Forgot Password?** if needed.
- Check the internet connection and retry.

### I did not receive an email or code

- Check Spam, Junk, and Promotions folders.
- Confirm that the Gmail address is correct.
- Wait briefly, then use the resend option.
- For guardian consent, confirm that the guardian is checking the email address entered during registration.

### The microphone does not work

- Allow microphone access in the browser prompt.
- Open browser site settings and change Microphone permission to **Allow**.
- Confirm that the correct input device is selected in the operating system.
- Close other applications using the microphone.
- Refresh V.E.R.A. and start a new session.
- Try Chrome or Edge if speech recognition is unavailable in the current browser.

### No speech was detected

- Move closer to the microphone.
- Speak after recording has started.
- Reduce background noise.
- Check that the device is not muted.
- Record a slightly longer message.

### The AI reply or voice is delayed

- Keep the page open while processing.
- Check the connection.
- Wait for the current request to finish before pressing the microphone again.
- If generated speech is unavailable, V.E.R.A. may use the browser's built-in voice.

### I cannot start Voice AI or Avatar AI

- Confirm that the account is signed in.
- Check the token balance.
- Voice AI requires 2 tokens.
- Avatar AI requires 3 tokens.
- Complete eligible activities if more tokens are needed.

### A chart or report is empty

- Record the related activity first.
- Select a wider time range.
- Refresh the page.
- Confirm that the account has saved entries for that period.

### A PDF does not download

- Allow downloads or pop-ups for the V.E.R.A. site.
- Retry after the page finishes loading.
- Check the browser's Downloads folder.

### The interface looks incorrect on mobile

- Refresh the page.
- Rotate the device or use portrait mode.
- Open navigation with the menu icon.
- Update the browser.

## 15. Privacy and Responsible Use

- Keep login and verification codes private.
- Sign out when using a shared device.
- Review the Store Conversations and AI Analysis preferences in Profile.
- Do not record another person without their informed permission.
- Avoid sharing passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or unnecessary identifying details with the AI.
- Treat exported reports as sensitive personal information.
- Administrators and psychology professionals must access user data only for authorized purposes.
- Do not use AI-generated responses or risk labels as a substitute for professional judgment.

## 16. Signing Out

1. Open the profile menu or mobile navigation.
2. Select **Logout**.
3. Wait until the landing page appears.

Signing out clears V.E.R.A.'s locally stored session information from that browser. Always sign out on a shared or public device.
